## 1. Licensing Models Overview

CodeX provides three licensing tiers designed for individual developers, organizations, and long-term licensees. Pricing, seat quotas, and technical service terms are determined exclusively through official CodeX distribution channels or written commercial agreements and are not hardcoded into this documentation.

### A. Individual Subscription

- **Billing Cadence:** Monthly or annual subscription plans.
- **Target Audience:** Individual developers for personal or authorized individual professional use.
- **Grant & Duration:** Non-exclusive, non-transferable usage rights remain active solely during an active, paid subscription period.
- **Expiration & Suspension:** Access and usage rights terminate or may be suspended upon expiration, cancellation without renewal, or breach of the [EULA](EULA.md).
- **Source & Redistribution:** Includes binary application usage only; grants no access to proprietary CodeX source code and prohibits redistribution, resale, or sublicensing.

### B. Enterprise License

- **Target Audience:** Businesses, companies, universities, government institutions, and commercial teams.
- **Commercial Terms:** Pricing, authorized user seats, device allocations, permitted deployment topologies, and internal distribution rights are defined in a separate signed Enterprise Agreement.
- **Integration & Deployment:** Organizational deployment, offline packaging, or custom workflow integration rights are strictly limited to what is expressly granted in the applicable agreement.
- **Support & SLA:** Optional priority support, custom onboarding, or service-level agreements (SLAs) are available only when explicitly included in the written Enterprise contract.

### C. Lifetime License

- **Payment Structure:** One-time purchase payment.
- **Grant & Duration:** Grants perpetual usage rights for the licensed version/scope, subject to ongoing compliance with the [EULA](EULA.md).
- **Exclusions Unless Written:** A Lifetime License does **not** automatically include perpetual feature updates, future major versions, ongoing technical support, or hosted/cloud services unless explicitly stated in writing at purchase.
- **Restrictions:** Remains closed-source and non-transferable; grants no source code ownership or redistribution rights.
